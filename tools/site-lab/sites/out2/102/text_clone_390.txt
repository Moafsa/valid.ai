Get started Frequently asked questions How is Bubble different from AI coding tools like Lovable, Bolt, or Replit? The core difference is what you get when the AI is done building: code or a visual app. Tools like Lovable, Bolt, and Replit generate traditional code, which means when something breaks or you want to make a change, you're stuck prompting and hoping or trying to edit the code yourself. Bubble generates apps in a visual programming language instead so you can understand and control everything without ever touching code. When you build with Bubble, everything is controlled visually — not only the design elements, but also the logic that controls how the app behaves, how your data is structured, and the privacy rules that dictate who can access what. You can chat with Bubble's AI Agent to add features and troubleshoot, or step into the visual editor to make precise changes yourself. You're never locked out of your own app. Can I build a real, production-ready app with Bubble — or just a prototype? You can build an app that’s ready to launch and grow — Bubble gives you everything you need to grow your app to millions of users and beyond. That includes a built-in database, user authentication, enterprise-grade hosting, automatic scaling, security scanning, and one-click deployment for web and native iOS and Android. You don't need to stitch together third-party tools or hire a DevOps team. What you build on Bubble is designed to grow with your business from day one. Nearly 6 million builders have used Bubble to launch apps that handle real users, real transactions, and real scale — Bubble apps process more than $1 billion in transactions per year. What kinds of apps have people built with Bubble? Builders have used Bubble to launch everything from SaaS startups and two-sided marketplaces to internal business tools, mobile apps, and industry-specific platforms. Real examples include fintech apps handling payment processing, healthcare platforms managing patient workflows, and solo founders who replaced enterprise software costing hundreds of thousands of dollars a year with custom Bubble apps.
