Case Study A Fusion of Elegance, Sophistication, and a Jazz-inspired Art Deco Atmosphere When crafting an identity for a jazz café named Decadance, I set out to create an identity that was sophisticated, rustic and inviting. The design also needed to capture the café’s unique blend of cool, elegance, and the timeless allure of jazz. To achieve this, I incorporated subtle Art Deco motifs and seamlessly integrated them into Toronto skyline. The design extended beyond just the logo, influencing everything from menu layouts to signage and website. Every element was carefully curated to ensure that the brand felt cohesive and luxurious, attracting both jazz aficionados and food connoisseurs looking for an elevated dining experience in the heart of Toronto. Visit Site Empowering Small Businesses for Growth My goal was to craft a strong visual identity and online presence for Talavera General Inc., a family-owned general renovation business that focused on quality and versatility. This sense of reliability extends into the website’s overall structure and user experience. Case Study Designing an Umbrella Network for a Roofing Company The idea was to centralize the structure of multiple roofing websites under a single framework, allowing each company to maintain its unique identity while benefiting from shared resources and consistent branding. This umbrella network enables easier updates, improved SEO, and cross-promotional opportunities across the companies. Leveraging modern web technologies like HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, I ensured the system is scalable, mobile-responsive, and optimized for both user experience and search engine visibility.
